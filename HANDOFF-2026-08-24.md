## 2. The three findings that matter

**(a) The managed oracle suppressed disputes ~26x, but did not remove them.**
0.113% of managed-oracle proposals are disputed (27/23,898 sampled across
Jan–Aug 2026) against a 2.90% plain-OOv2 baseline from our own 2024 data. UMA's
proposer whitelist, enforced 2025-09-05, is the documented cause. Absolute
disputes *rose* ~9x over the same period because proposal volume grew ~237x.
Extrapolates to ~2,000–2,600 disputes in the sanity gate's Jan–May window
against a ~1,000 threshold, so **the gate should pass**. ADR-0017.

**(b) Listing-time text carries a real but modest signal.** 170 markets judged
blind by the website session against a criterion pre-registered before the
input existed. Excluding items the judge recognised: contested 44.1% ambiguous
vs control 30.1% — **+14.0pp, lift 1.46x, p≈0.075**. Restricted to confident
judgements: +20.5pp, 1.78x, p≈0.026. The effect sharpens under a confidence
restriction, which is how a real signal behaves.

The uncomfortable half: **~30% of non-disputed markets also carry visible
ambiguity.** Ambiguity is common; disputes are rare. At a ~0.1% base rate a
perfect binary ambiguity screen would flag a third of the corpus to catch under
half the disputes. **Text alone cannot be the headline claim** — the product
has to be stakes-first with text as a secondary signal.

Note the study deliberately volume-matched its controls, so it measures text's
marginal contribution *at constant stakes*. That is the right question for "does
text carry signal" but it understates a model combining text with stakes, and
stakes is the stronger predictor. Re-run both ways once labels exist.

**(c) Linter v1 is weak, and we know precisely how.** Corpus-wide:
`hedge-words` 57.3%, `no-na-condition` 40.9%, `vague-source` 9.5%,
`deadline-no-timezone` 1.0%, `status-verb-gap` 0.8%,
`occurrence-vs-reporting` 0.1%, `outcomes-not-exhaustive` **0.0% — zero hits in
2.6M versions**. Only 10.8% of versions have a hit beyond the top two. And on
the June 2026 Strategy market — a $60M+ dispute decided on sale-date vs
disclosure-date, textbook `occurrence-vs-reporting` — **the rule did not fire**.

## 3. What was built

**Correctness**
- **ADR-0014** — V4 adapter addresses had invalid EIP-55 checksums. viem
  rejects a bad-checksum literal on `readContract` but `getLogs` lowercases
  first, so event queries worked while every contract read threw. That hid
  MOOv2 for a day and would have recorded **zero disputes for ~1.88M markets**.
  Fixed; a test now pins every address as checksummed.
- **MOOv2 identified** at `0x2C0367a9DB231dDeBd88a94b4f6461a6e47C58B1`, read
  from the adapters' own immutable. `MOOV2_ADDRESS` stays unset.
- Requester coverage verified **100%** in both 2024 and 2026 — no unindexed
  adapter, no OOReporter blind spot (yet; recheck before each backfill).
- `questionID = keccak256(ancillaryData)` verified: 159/159 dispute questionIds
  match an adapter-reported id. The 53% market-join rate is the 2024 corpus cut,
  not a bug, and `REPORT.md` now discloses it.

**Cost and robustness** (ADR-0013, 0015, 0016, 0018, 0019)
- Infura-only transport for deep sweeps; **Infura caps `eth_getLogs` at 10,000
  blocks regardless of result size** (measured — this corrects ADR-0002 and is
  the floor under every cost estimate).
- One `getLogs` for all three OO events via manual topics: ~40% fewer credits.
  Verified by running both forms over three real ranges — identical sets,
  8,316 logs.
- Adaptive range now remembers what failed instead of re-probing it; rate
  limits back off in time rather than shrinking; **network outages are waited
  out** rather than killing the run.
- Linter, labels and export all stream now. The previous shape needed several
  GB and would have died at the end of a day-long job.
- `--reset-cursor` and `--from-block` are audited code paths, not DB edits.

**Exporter** — `volume_decile`, `venue_id`, `closed_time`, `outcomes`,
`outcome_prices`, `label_computed_at`. REPORT.md prints contested rate by
decile.

## 4. Known traps

- **`hit_*` columns are a latest-text view, not listing-time.** Anything
  claiming to be hindsight-free must join `rules_versions WHERE version_num = 1`.
  They diverge exactly on rules-edited markets, in the flattering direction.
- **`key.json` sits next to the judge's input** in `data/blind/`. Move it before
  any future round.
- **Gamma's `volume` may count both sides.** Doesn't affect `volume_decile`
  (rank-based) but does affect absolute dollar claims.
- **`data/blind/` is gitignored** — rebuildable, and it holds verbatim rules
  text plus the answers.
- Machine sleep is the only unrecoverable failure mode, and it has now killed
  two runs.

### P0.2 Re-run the signal analysis with real power
The blind study had 85 positives. After the backfill there are ~1,150 —
**13x**. Redo it three ways and treat the result as the real answer:
1. volume-matched (comparable to today's 1.46x),
2. unmatched (text + stakes together, the product-relevant number),
3. conditioned on `volume_decile` — the cut that decides whether a watchlist is
   sellable.

### P0.3 Linter v1.1 — three named fixes
- Retire or repair `outcomes-not-exhaustive` (0 hits in 2.6M).
- Rewrite `occurrence-vs-reporting` with the Strategy market as a fixture. It is
  the best ground-truth case we have: high-profile, expensive, independently
  adjudicated, and its cause maps to one named rule.
- Demote or reweight `hedge-words` / `no-na-condition`. Confirm with lift once
  labels exist, but a rule firing on 57% of the corpus cannot rank a 0.1% event.
- Aim at the kinds that actually discriminated (`subj`, `oth`, `edge`); `src`
  and `thr` were near-even and would add noise.

### P0.4 Resolve the volume semantics
Settle whether Gamma's `volume` double-counts. Gates every absolute figure the
website wants to publish; does not gate deciles.

### P1 Product and infrastructure
- `site-export.ts` emitting the four JSON files, `rules_text` for the contested
  subset only, and `contentHash` (spec accepted: SHA-256 over JCS, we emit
  canonical bytes, anchored via `appendAudit`).
- **Move long jobs off the laptop.** Not needed for the backfill if sleep is
  disabled, but Phase 3's re-poll worker must run continuously over calendar
  time to give `rules_edited_after_listing` any signal at all — a laptop cannot
  do that. This is the real argument for a VPS.
- Decide the `/eval` split deliberately: whitelist enforcement (2025-09-05) sits
